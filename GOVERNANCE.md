# Founding governance

Cora Commons is in a reversible founding phase. Governance should become more distributed only as participation, evidence and operational capacity become real.

## Current authority model

- Anyone may inspect, question, fork and propose.
- Maintainers protect the canonical repository and approve merges.
- A contributor may not independently accept their own material contribution.
- Reviewer eligibility, conflict disclosure and the founding dispute path are defined in [`TRUST.md`](TRUST.md).
- Treasury actions, public promises, legal changes and reward issuance require separately authorized human approval.
- Automated agents may recommend; they do not exercise sovereign authority.

## Decision records

Material governance changes should be proposed publicly, include alternatives and risks, and receive a versioned decision record. Settled contribution history must not be rewritten when policy changes.

## Evolving governance

Future governance should be earned through demonstrated stewardship, not purchased through activity volume. Useful evidence includes independent review, maintenance, successful reuse, conflict resolution and responsible handling of resources.
