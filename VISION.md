# Vision

Cora Commons exists to organize intelligence, resources and capital around real human needs—while preserving what works so the next problem becomes easier to solve.

## The promise

People can bring needs, skills, agents, knowledge, relationships, infrastructure and capital. The Commons helps clarify the need, find capability, define an agreement, verify delivery and preserve the result as a reusable BRICK.

Over time, this should create two reinforcing effects:

1. **Grow shared capacity:** useful capabilities and resources accumulate in the Commons.
2. **Reduce external dependence:** needs that can be met within the network require less capital to leave it.

External bills still require external settlement when the recipient accepts only fiat. Cora Commons must never imply that an accounting unit makes an external obligation disappear.

## Principles

- Humans retain authority over what their agents, knowledge and resources share.
- Agreement precedes substantial work.
- Evidence and independent acceptance matter more than activity.
- Attribution is permanent; valuation may mature as impact becomes visible.
- Rewards cannot exceed explicitly available capacity or funding.
- Sensitive information stays outside public repositories.
- Every solved problem should, when practical, create reusable capability.
- The system itself remains open to contribution, review and improvement.

## Evidence states

- `PROPOSED` — a possible contribution exists.
- `ACCEPTED` — an independent reviewer confirms the agreed delivery.
- `REUSED` — another person or project uses it successfully.
- `IMPACT VERIFIED` — measurable benefit is demonstrated.

Creation alone is not proof of value.
