# Agent Commons — how it works (v1.0, November 2026)

> This page is the current design. Where older pages in this repo (for example REWARDS.md or CAPITAL_COMMONS.md) differ, this page governs while they are being updated.

Every bottleneck we hit becomes an agent. Every agent we prove joins the Commons, so the next person doesn't start from zero.

## The offer

In one week, free, we build you an AI agent that takes a recurring job off your plate for good. Then for $150 a month, we keep building together: your agents grow, you learn to run them, and you're part of a community building agents for each other.

- Month to month. Your founding rate stays while you're a member.
- You approve anything that sends, posts or spends. Low-risk work runs on its own and is reported to you.
- Your data stays in your own accounts. Nothing is published or sent without your approval.

## Why it exists

Most people don't need more AI tools. They need someone who turns AI into actual relief. The biggest barrier isn't budget; it's not knowing where to start, not having time, and not trusting tools that overpromise. We start it for you, and we prove it works.

## How the Commons grows

1. **Bottleneck** → we write a playbook for solving it.
2. **Playbook** → an agent is built to run it, then independently verified.
3. **Proof** → it runs on real work, with evidence.
4. **BRICK** → once proven and approved, it's published here as a portable skill folder (instructions, scripts, MCP config) that members can copy.
5. **Improve** → real results and outside research update it; every change is versioned.

## Proof before claims

We say only what's proven. Anything still being designed is labeled a blueprint. We measure one thing above all: recurring jobs taken off people's plates, verified still running.

## Contributors

Builders, content creators, attorneys and CPAs who help the Commons grow can earn usage credits, early access, a free build of their own agent, and the affiliate commission below. Each contributor has a short written agreement.

## Affiliates

- Members can share the offer and earn 30% of a referred member's payments for their first 12 months.
- Single level only. Paid only on real customer purchases. Never for signups or recruiting. No buy-in.
- Affiliates disclose that they're paid. No income or earnings claims.

## Credits

Usage credits are non-transferable, non-cashable and capped. They're for using the network, not an investment. Anything beyond this waits for licensed legal review.

## Safety

Agents work with least privilege, treat outside content as data rather than instructions, have cost and action limits, and escalate money, sending, permissions and legal matters to a human.
