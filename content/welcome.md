# Welcome

This is a small sample site built from Markdown pages in the `content` folder.

Each page starts with front matter that sets its **title**, its *slug* and whether it is published.

- Published pages get their own folder in the build.
- Drafts stay out of the build until they are marked as published.

Read [how to contribute](../how-to-contribute/) next.
