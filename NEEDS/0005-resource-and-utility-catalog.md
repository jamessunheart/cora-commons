# Need 0005 — Verified resource and utility catalog

**Priority:** P1 — build after evidence foundation  
**Depends on:** Needs 0002–0004  
**Unlocks:** real Capital Commons inventory and bounded CORA utility

## Outcome

Participants can discover resources that are genuinely available to fulfill Needs or redeem under explicit agreements.

## Initial resource classes

- model/API or GPU capacity;
- professional services and human time;
- software and BRICKS;
- Zen Village sessions, nights or workshop seats;
- equipment, space and logistics;
- sponsored or donated fiat budgets with stated restrictions.

## Acceptance evidence

- Offers record owner/steward, quantity or capacity, restrictions, validity window, fulfillment method and evidence.
- Resource states follow `DECLARED -> VERIFIED -> COMMITTED -> RESERVED -> DELIVERED -> RECONCILED`.
- Expired, depleted and restricted capacity cannot appear available.
- Public records contain no credentials, private beneficiary details or payment secrets.
- One test offer can be reserved, delivered and reconciled without double allocation.

## Useful contributions

- catalog schema;
- availability and reservation logic;
- provider verification;
- privacy review;
- first bounded utility offers.
