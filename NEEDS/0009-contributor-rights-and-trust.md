# Need 0009 — Contributor Rights & Trust Protocol

**Priority:** P0 — resolve before accepting high-value BRICKS  
**Depends on:** Needs 0003 and 0004  
**Unlocks:** safer contribution of intellectual property, credible review and bounded economic pilots

## Outcome

A participant can understand what they retain, what they license, who may independently review their work, how a disputed decision is appealed and exactly what real utility backs any pilot reward.

## Acceptance evidence

- Four realistic contribution scenarios—code, business knowledge, private data and a physical/service offer—produce an unambiguous rights result.
- A reviewer eligibility check detects contributor overlap, direct reward interest and disclosed reciprocal or relationship conflicts.
- A public-safe dispute can move through filing, recusal, evidence review and a recorded outcome without rewriting history.
- A pilot disclosure names the resource pool, quantity or cap, custodian, access rule, depletion condition and settlement evidence.
- The policy receives independent contributor review and qualified legal review before regulated activity or material intellectual property relies on it.
- Plain-language onboarding states that current participation earns attribution only unless a reward is separately agreed and backed.

## Useful contributions

- review and improve [`TRUST.md`](../TRUST.md);
- contributor-rights decision tree;
- conflict-of-interest declaration and deterministic checks;
- dispute issue form and append-only decision schema;
- pilot-backing disclosure template;
- jurisdiction-specific legal review questions.

## Current boundary

The repository's founding policy clarifies immediate participation but is not a final legal framework. Do not contribute confidential, restricted or high-value proprietary material merely because this Need exists.
