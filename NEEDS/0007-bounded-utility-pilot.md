# Need 0007 — Bounded Capital Commons utility pilot

**Priority:** P1 — after catalog and simulator  
**Depends on:** Needs 0002–0006  
**Unlocks:** evidence that contribution, utility and replenishment form a real loop

## Outcome

A small group completes one full cycle: contribute verified value, receive an explicitly funded or service-backed allocation, use it for a real utility, and record fulfillment and replenishment separately.

## Acceptance evidence

- The sponsor/resource cap and fulfillment limits are published before participation.
- At least one intelligence utility and one non-compute utility are available.
- Participants can see whether value is provisional, funded, available, reserved or settled.
- The pilot records contribution, allocation, redemption, provider fulfillment and Commons replenishment as distinct events.
- No participant is promised value beyond funded/service-backed capacity.
- Results separately measure agent return, exchange activity, fulfillment cost, reuse and participant understanding.

## Useful contributions

- pilot design;
- initial sponsored utility;
- fulfillment operations;
- participant testing;
- accounting and outcome review.
