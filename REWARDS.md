# Contribution recognition and rewards

For the member offer and how the Agent Commons works, see [AGENT-COMMONS.md](AGENT-COMMONS.md).

## Founding rule

Every accepted contribution should preserve attribution. Attribution does not by itself create a payable debt, redeemable balance, ownership interest or guaranteed CORA allocation.

## Reward stages

1. `RECORDED` — contributor, artifact and timestamp are preserved.
2. `ACCEPTED` — an independent reviewer accepts the agreed delivery.
3. `REUSED` — another participant successfully uses the contribution.
4. `IMPACT VERIFIED` — measurable benefit, cost reduction or new capacity is demonstrated.
5. `REWARD ALLOCATED` — authorized governance allocates an explicitly funded or service-backed return.

## Retroactive recognition

The historical contribution record remains immutable. A versioned reward policy may add recognition or later allocations as evidence of reuse and impact appears. Legitimately settled rewards are not repriced or clawed back merely because a later valuation policy changes. Fraud, duplicate claims and overturned acceptance follow the founding process in [`TRUST.md`](TRUST.md) until a dedicated dispute service is accepted.

## What is not rewarded automatically

- uploads;
- raw activity or commit counts;
- unrequested work;
- self-accepted work;
- unverifiable claims;
- duplicated or generated volume without demonstrated usefulness.

Potential reward inputs include agreed scope, independent acceptance, reuse, impact, maintenance burden, dependencies unlocked, cost consumed and contribution from other participants.

## Usage credits

Usage credits are non-transferable, non-cashable and capped. They pay for using the network's services; they are not an investment, a security or a claim on funds. Converting credits to cash, trading them, or any multi-level reward is not offered and would require licensed legal review first.

## Affiliate commission

Members may share the Agent Commons offer and earn 30% of a referred member's payments for that member's first 12 months.

- Single level only.
- Paid only on real customer purchases, never for signups or recruiting.
- No buy-in is required to participate.
- Affiliates disclose that they are paid when they share.
- No one makes income or earnings claims.
